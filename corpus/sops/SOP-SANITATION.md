## 1. Purpose
Routine sanitation of production lines.

## 2. Frequency
2.1 Full wet sanitation at the end of each production day.
2.2 Mid-shift dry sanitation where run length exceeds 10 hours.
2.3 Wet allergen wash whenever required by SOP-ALLERGEN-CO section 4; this is
an allergen control, not a routine sanitation activity, and is recorded
separately.

## 3. Sequence
3.1 Isolate and lock out. 3.2 Dry clean and remove gross soil. 3.3 Pre-rinse.
3.4 Detergent application and contact time. 3.5 Rinse. 3.6 Sanitiser.
3.7 Inspection and sign-off.

## 4. Duration
4.1 Full wet sanitation is scheduled at 60 to 90 minutes per line and is
planned downtime, recorded under reason code SANITATION.

4.2 Sanitation overrunning its window displaces production and is raised at
the daily review.

## 5. Verification
5.1 ATP swabs at defined points following sanitation. Failing points are
re-cleaned and re-swabbed before production.

## 6. Records
Sanitation records and swab results retained 24 months.
