## 1. Purpose
Step sequence for a changeover where the incoming SKU introduces no new
allergen.

## 2. Eligibility
2.1 Permitted only after an allergen determination under SOP-ALLERGEN-CO
section 2. If any allergen is introduced, a wet wash is required and this
procedure does not apply.

## 3. Sequence
3.1 Run out the outgoing SKU and clear the line of product.
3.2 Change forming tooling and adjust settings to the incoming SKU recipe.
3.3 Dry brush-down of product-contact surfaces and removal of visible debris.
3.4 Update the packaging film and date coding, and verify the code is correct
before the first pack.
3.5 Purge the first units to scrap under CHANGEOVER_PURGE.
3.6 QA line clearance check and sign-off.

## 4. Duration
4.1 Scheduled durations range from 22 to 50 minutes depending on tooling
complexity and are held per line and SKU in the production system.

4.2 Changeovers consistently exceeding their scheduled duration are raised at
the daily operations review. A rising changeover count on a line is a
scheduling signal, not a line performance signal.

## 5. Records
Changeover record retained 36 months.
