## 1. Purpose
To control product and materials whose conformance is in question.

## 2. Placing a hold
2.1 Any employee may place a hold. No approval is required to hold product;
approval is required only to release it.

2.2 The hold is recorded with the affected lot or batch codes, the reason,
and the time it was placed, before the product is physically moved.

2.3 Held product is physically segregated and labelled. Segregation by
paperwork alone is not sufficient.

## 3. Common triggers
3.1 Failed metal detector challenge test -- see HACCP-METAL section 5.

3.2 Suspected allergen cross-contact, including any changeover where swab
results are outstanding.

3.3 Out-of-specification bake, weight, or dimension results.

3.4 Non-food-grade lubricant or chemical contact with a product-contact
surface.

## 4. Release
4.1 Only the QA shift lead or the food safety manager may release held
product.

4.2 Release requires a documented rationale referencing test results or
inspection findings. "Subsequent production passed" is not a release
rationale for previously held product.

4.3 Product held for allergen cross-contact is released only on passing swab
results covering the specific line and time period in question.

## 5. Rejection
5.1 Product that cannot be released is dispositioned to feed or waste under
SOP-SCRAP-DISP section 5 and recorded as QUALITY_REJECT.

## 6. Records
Hold register retained 36 months.
