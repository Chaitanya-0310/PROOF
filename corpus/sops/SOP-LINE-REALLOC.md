## 1. Purpose
To define who may move a scheduled production run to a different line, and
what must be confirmed before the move.

## 2. When reallocation is considered
2.1 A run may be reallocated when its scheduled line is unavailable and a
compatible line has capacity within the affected shipping window.

2.2 Reallocation is a recovery action, not a scheduling convenience. Routine
schedule changes follow the weekly planning process, not this procedure.

## 3. Approval authority
3.1 **Within a plant, same shift:** the plant manager approves. The shift
supervisor may initiate but may not approve their own request.

3.2 **Across plants:** requires the regional operations director. Cross-plant
moves change the shipping origin and must be confirmed with logistics before
approval.

3.3 **Where the move affects a strategic-tier customer commitment:** the
commercial account owner must be notified before the move is executed, not
after. See POL-CUSTOMER-NOTIF section 4.

3.4 No reallocation is executed on the authority of a planning system
recommendation alone. A named person approves every move.

## 4. Pre-move confirmations
4.1 **Allergen compatibility.** Confirm under SOP-ALLERGEN-CO whether the
receiving line requires a wet allergen wash. If it does, the wash duration is
added to the changeover and the recovery estimate must be revised before
approval, not after.

4.2 **Rate difference.** Receiving lines frequently run the same SKU at a
different rate. Recompute the completion time using the receiving line's
rate; do not assume the originating line's schedule holds.

4.3 **Changeover cost.** The receiving line's changeover time is lost
production on that line. Confirm what is displaced and whether that
displacement creates a second shortfall.

4.4 **Staged WIP.** Dough already staged on the stopped line cannot be
transferred unless its remaining proof window exceeds the transfer plus
changeover duration. See SOP-PROOF-WINDOW section 6.3.

## 5. Execution
5.1 The approved move is recorded in the production system before the
receiving line begins changeover.

5.2 Run-to-order allocations are updated at the same time. A reallocation
that moves production without moving the order allocation produces a shortfall
that is invisible until the truck is loading.

## 6. Records
The reallocation record captures the originating downtime event, the
approver, the allergen determination, and the revised completion estimate.
Retained 24 months.
