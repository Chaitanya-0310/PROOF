## 1. Purpose
To maintain one-up one-down traceability for all materials and finished
product.

## 2. Requirement
2.1 Every finished lot traces back to the raw material lots consumed, the
line, the run, and the shift.

2.2 Every raw material lot traces forward to all finished lots that consumed
it.

2.3 Traceability is reconstructable within 4 hours of a request at any time.

## 3. Lot coding
3.1 Finished product carries a date code and line identifier sufficient to
identify the run.

3.2 Raw material lot codes are captured at the point of staging, not at
goods-in. Staging is where the material is committed to a run.

## 4. Inter-plant transfers
4.1 Material transferred between plants retains its original supplier lot
code. Re-coding at the receiving plant breaks the chain to the supplier and
is prohibited.

4.2 Transfers require goods-in QA acceptance at the receiving plant.

## 5. Mock recall
5.1 Conducted twice per year, covering one forward and one backward trace.

5.2 Target is 100 percent of product accounted for within 4 hours. A result
below 99 percent triggers a corrective action plan.

## 6. Records
Traceability records retained 36 months or shelf life plus 12 months,
whichever is longer.
