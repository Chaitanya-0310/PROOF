## 1. Purpose
To define the checks required before a flatbread line resumes production
following an unplanned stoppage.

## 2. Scope
All flatbread lines. For sweet goods lines see SOP-RESTART-SWEET; the
validation sequence differs and the two are not interchangeable.

## 3. Authority to restart
3.1 The line may not restart until the shift supervisor and a QA technician
have both signed the restart record.

3.2 For stoppages exceeding 60 minutes, the QA shift lead must also sign.

## 4. Pre-restart checks
4.1 **Mechanical clearance.** Maintenance confirms in writing that the fault
is cleared and the guard interlocks have been reinstated. For sheeter faults
specifically, follow WI-SHEETER section 5 before requesting clearance.

4.2 **Proof window verification.** Identify every batch still staged on the
line. Batches that have exceeded their proof window are removed and scrapped
under SOP-PROOF-WINDOW section 5 BEFORE the line restarts. Running an
over-proofed batch through the oven to "see how it comes out" is prohibited.

4.3 **Oven profile confirmation.** Verify that zone temperatures have
recovered to the SKU set point and have held for a minimum of 10 minutes.
Flatbread lines lose deck temperature quickly when the belt stops.

4.4 **Foreign material sweep.** Visually inspect the sheeter, the belt, and
the transfer points for tooling, fasteners, or debris introduced during the
intervention.

## 5. Startup validation
5.1 The first 3 minutes of output following any stoppage over 15 minutes is
diverted to scrap under reason code STARTUP_LOSS. It is not packed.

5.2 A QA technician takes a sample at 3 minutes and at 10 minutes and checks
weight, dimension, and bake colour against specification.

5.3 If either sample fails, the line stops again and the QA shift lead is
notified. Do not adjust and continue without a fresh sample.

## 6. Metal detection
6.1 The metal detector must be challenge-tested with the full test piece set
before packed product is released. See HACCP-METAL section 6.

6.2 Product produced between restart and a passing challenge test is held,
not released.

## 7. Records
The restart record captures the downtime event reference, both signatures,
sample results, and the scrap quantity diverted at startup. Retained 24
months.
