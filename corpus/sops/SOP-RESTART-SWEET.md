## 1. Purpose
To define the checks required before a sweet goods line resumes production
following an unplanned stoppage.

## 2. Scope
All sweet goods lines. This procedure is not interchangeable with
SOP-RESTART-FLAT; fryer and glazing controls have no flatbread equivalent.

## 3. Time criticality
3.1 Sweet goods carry a 70 minute nominal proof window, the shortest of any
category. Staged product is lost faster here than anywhere else in the
network.

3.2 The supervisor establishes the staged batch position and remaining
expiry time within 5 minutes of the stoppage, before diagnosis of the fault
is complete.

## 4. Pre-restart checks
4.1 **Fryer temperature.** Confirm oil temperature has recovered to set
point and has held for 15 minutes. Frying below set point produces excessive
oil pickup and is a consumer complaint driver.

4.2 **Oil quality.** Where the stoppage exceeded 45 minutes, take a polar
compounds reading. Oil held hot without throughput degrades faster than oil
in production.

4.3 **Proof window verification.** Remove and scrap all batches past their
window under SOP-PROOF-WINDOW section 5 before restart.

4.4 **Glaze and topping systems.** Confirm glaze temperature and viscosity.
Glaze held static will skin and must be recirculated before use.

## 5. Startup validation
5.1 The first 5 minutes of output after any stoppage over 15 minutes is
diverted to scrap under STARTUP_LOSS. Sweet goods carry a longer startup
diversion than flatbread because fryer and glaze systems stabilise more
slowly.

5.2 QA samples at 5 and 15 minutes for weight, moisture, and glaze coverage.

## 6. Metal detection
As SOP-RESTART-FLAT section 6. Challenge test before release; hold product
produced before a passing test.

## 7. Records
Retained 24 months.
