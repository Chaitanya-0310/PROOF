## 1. Purpose
To prevent allergen cross-contact when changing production from one SKU to
another on the same line.

## 2. Principle
2.1 The controlling question is whether the incoming SKU introduces an
allergen the line was not already running. It is the DIRECTION of the change
that matters, not the number of allergens involved.

2.2 Moving from a product containing milk and egg to a wheat-only product
introduces no new allergen and requires only a dry changeover. The reverse
introduces milk and egg and requires a full wet wash.

2.3 Wheat is present in all products at these sites and is declared on all
labels; it is therefore not treated as an introduced allergen for changeover
purposes.

## 3. Dry changeover
3.1 Permitted only when the incoming SKU introduces no new allergen.

3.2 Dry changeover comprises tooling change, dry brush-down of contact
surfaces, purge of the first units, and line clearance check.

3.3 Nominal duration is the scheduled changeover time held in the production
system, typically 22 to 50 minutes depending on the SKU.

## 4. Wet allergen wash
4.1 Required whenever the incoming SKU introduces any allergen not already
running on the line.

4.2 The wash comprises full disassembly of product-contact tooling, hot
caustic wash, potable rinse, sanitiser application, and a documented
post-wash inspection.

4.3 **Duration.** A wet allergen wash adds a minimum of 45 minutes to the
scheduled dry changeover time, and commonly 60 to 90 minutes on sweet goods
lines where the depositor and glazing equipment must be stripped. Recovery
estimates that use the scheduled changeover figure alone will be wrong by
roughly an hour.

4.4 **Verification.** Protein-residue swabs are taken at the defined contact
points. Production does not begin until all swabs return a pass. A visual
inspection is not a substitute for swab results.

## 5. Changeover purge
5.1 The first units after any changeover are diverted to scrap under reason
code CHANGEOVER_PURGE regardless of changeover type.

5.2 Frequent changeovers therefore carry a fixed scrap cost per occurrence.
Where changeover frequency on a line rises, purge scrap rises proportionally
and this should be investigated as a scheduling issue rather than a line
performance issue.

## 6. Records
Changeover records capture the outgoing and incoming SKU, the allergen
determination, the changeover type, swab results where applicable, and the
purge quantity. Retained 36 months.
