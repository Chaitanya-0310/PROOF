## 1. Hazard
Biological hazard: survival of vegetative pathogens through insufficient
thermal processing.

## 2. CCP designation
The bake or fry step is a Critical Control Point and is the kill step for
these products.

## 3. Critical limits
3.1 Minimum internal temperature 85 C held at the product centre at oven
exit for baked products.

3.2 Minimum oil temperature 175 C throughout the fry residence time for fried
products.

3.3 Zone set points and belt speed per the SKU bake profile.

## 4. Monitoring
4.1 Continuous zone temperature logging with alarm on deviation.

4.2 Probe check of product centre temperature at start of run, every 2 hours,
and after any stoppage exceeding 15 minutes.

## 5. Corrective action
5.1 Product produced during a period of deviation is held under
SOP-HOLD-RELEASE.

5.2 The line does not restart until zone temperatures have recovered to set
point and held for the period defined in the applicable restart SOP -- 10
minutes for flatbread, 15 minutes for sweet goods fryers.

5.3 Restarting on a recovered set point reading alone, without the hold
period, is a critical non-conformance. Deck and oil temperature readings
recover faster than the mass of the equipment.

## 6. Verification
Annual thermal validation of each oven and fryer. Weekly record review.

## 7. Records
Retained 36 months.
