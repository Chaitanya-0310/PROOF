## 1. Hazard
Physical hazard: metal fragments introduced by equipment wear, maintenance
activity, or raw material.

## 2. CCP designation
Metal detection at the packing line is a Critical Control Point. Loss of
control requires product hold and a documented decision before release.

## 3. Critical limits
3.1 Ferrous 2.0 mm, non-ferrous 2.5 mm, stainless steel 3.0 mm, tested with
the certified test piece set for the product and pack format.

## 4. Monitoring
4.1 Challenge test at start of run, at each shift change, every 2 hours
during the run, at end of run, and after any line stoppage exceeding 15
minutes.

4.2 Each test passes all three test pieces. A single failed piece fails the
test.

## 5. Corrective action
5.1 On a failed challenge test, all product produced since the last passing
test is placed on hold under SOP-HOLD-RELEASE.

5.2 The detector is investigated and repaired. Production does not resume
until a full passing challenge test is recorded.

5.3 Held product is inspected or destroyed on the decision of the food safety
manager. It is not released on the basis of a subsequent passing test alone.

## 6. Post-stoppage requirement
6.1 Any stoppage over 15 minutes, planned or unplanned, requires a passing
challenge test before packed product is released.

6.2 Product produced between restart and the passing test is held, not
released. This applies even where the stoppage had no mechanical cause.

## 7. Verification
Weekly review of monitoring records by the food safety manager. Annual
validation of the test piece set.

## 8. Records
Monitoring records, corrective action records, and hold decisions. Retained
36 months.
